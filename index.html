    Find free songs from around the world

    Download a song, then add it above. For tracks you did not make, check the license first: public domain and CC0 are the safest, CC BY needs a credit.

    • Free Music Archive: free downloads in many genres, each with its own license.
    • Internet Archive Audio: public domain and Creative Commons recordings, including folk and traditional music.
    • Musopen: public domain classical recordings.
    • Wikimedia Commons: freely licensed audio, including traditional music from many countries.

    Mood music and world styles are composed live in your browser. Your own songs stay on your device and are gone when you close this page.
